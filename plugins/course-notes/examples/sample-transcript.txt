Now here's the part that confuses everybody, and it's the resizing settings. You'll see width and height

Fixed means it stays exactly the size you set, no matter what's inside.

Hug contents means the frame shrinks or grows to wrap tightly around whatever is inside it. That's what you

And fill container means stretch to fill whatever space the parent frame gives you. So if this button

A really common mistake is people set everything to fixed and then wonder why their auto layout isn't doing
